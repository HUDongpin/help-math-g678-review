## Source warning requiring explicit disposition

The independent binary parser and FFDec establish all declared tags through each End. Sprite 83 has 386 trailing bytes after End at uncompressed offset 100,601, SHA-256 `d14a608f74eab33aa50401e45f0226b845a8b29aa02417254d15dc8675accaa0`. Tagged extraction is therefore not proof of complete original-runtime audio semantics. The source audit retains the warning and its effect on claims.
